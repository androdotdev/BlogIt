BlogIt privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

BlogIt is a Chrome extension that turns an article page into a clean reading view. This policy explains what it does with your data. The short version: it collects nothing, sends nothing, and keeps everything on your device.

What BlogIt does not do

  • It has no server, no account system, and no sync.
  • It has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • It does not collect, sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not read your browsing history or run on pages you have not asked it to read.

What BlogIt reads

When you click the toolbar icon or press Alt + R, BlogIt reads the text and images of the page in the current tab. It runs Mozilla Readability on a copy of the page in your browser to find the article, then shows the result in an overlay. That processing happens entirely on your device. The page content is not uploaded anywhere.

What BlogIt stores

BlogIt stores two things, both in chrome.storage.local on your device:

  • Your settings: theme (light or dark) and text size.
  • Articles you choose to save: title, author, site name, excerpt, reading time, the cleaned article text, the page address, and a lead image address.

Articles are saved only when you press save. You can remove one at any time from the reader or the popup, and the popup has a clear-all option. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Images and links

Saved and displayed articles keep their original image addresses. When you read an article, your browser loads those images from the original site's servers, the same way it would on the original page. BlogIt asks the browser not to send a referrer with these requests. It does not proxy or copy images. Links inside articles open in a new tab on the original site.

Permissions

Permission Why BlogIt needs it
activeTab Lets BlogIt read the current tab, only after you click the icon or use the shortcut. It gives no access to other tabs.
scripting Injects the reader script into that tab when you ask for reader mode.
storage Keeps your settings and saved articles on your device.

BlogIt requests no host permissions and has no always-on content scripts.

Third parties

BlogIt includes open source libraries (Mozilla Readability, DOMPurify, marked, Turndown, React, and Radix Themes) bundled inside the extension. It does not load code from remote servers, and none of these libraries send data anywhere.

Children

BlogIt collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If BlogIt ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the date above will change. Any change that involves collecting or sending data would also be described in the extension's store listing.
